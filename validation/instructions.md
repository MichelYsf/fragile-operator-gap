# Second‑Coder Validation Instructions

Thank you for serving as an independent coder for this study.  Your task is to apply the five‑step assumption‑extraction protocol to a small stratified sample of normative statements from four cybersecurity frameworks.  You do not need to read the entire frameworks or the full paper.

## Materials provided

- `sample_cells.csv`: A subset of framework principles and their original text.  Each row contains:
  - `Framework`: the source document;
  - `Principle`: the identifier or clause name;
  - `Text`: the original normative statement.
- `five_step_protocol.md`: the protocol outlining the coding procedure.

## Coding tasks

1. For each row in `sample_cells.csv`, read the normative statement in the `Text` column.
2. Follow **Step 2** of the protocol to extract the directive language.
3. Follow **Step 3** to identify one or more implicit assumptions necessary for compliance.
4. Follow **Step 4** to map each assumption to one or more of the seven environmental dimensions.
5. Record your coded assumption(s) and mapped dimension(s) in a new spreadsheet column (`Coder_Assumptions` and `Coder_Dimensions`).  Do not consult the original author’s coding.

## Deliverable

Return your completed spreadsheet along with any notes or uncertainties.  The study team will compute inter‑coder agreement (Cohen’s κ) and document disagreements transparently.
